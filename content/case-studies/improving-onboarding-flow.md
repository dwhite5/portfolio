## The Problem

Only 40% of users who started the signup flow completed it. Session recordings showed people abandoning at a form asking for information they didn't have on hand — and no way to skip or come back.

## Approach

I audited every field in the flow and asked "what breaks if we don't collect this at signup?" Most fields could be deferred. I redesigned around a three-step core — email, password, one key preference — and moved everything else into a post-signup checklist users could complete at their own pace.

## What I Learned

Completion rates went up, but more importantly the quality of the data improved. When users filled in the deferred fields voluntarily, they filled them in accurately. Forced input at signup was producing junk data anyway.
