## The Problem

Users were overwhelmed by a dense data table that showed everything at once but made it hard to act on anything. Support tickets about "not knowing what to do next" were climbing.

## Approach

I started by shadowing three users during their daily routines to understand what decisions they actually needed to make. From there I sketched a hierarchy of information — surfacing the two or three signals that drove 80% of their actions, and pushing everything else into a detail view.

## What I Learned

The hardest part wasn't the design — it was convincing stakeholders that hiding information wasn't the same as losing it. Progressive disclosure only works if people trust that the detail is still there.
